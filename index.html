Hi, I'm MILKY

I've been obsessed with music since I was young. A school friend nicknamed me DJ because I was always the one making the playlists for parties and the hockey bus, and it stuck.

I'm a queer DJ in Glasgow and I've been behind the decks for almost 20 years. I used to run my own club nights here, and I've played hundreds of parties, weddings, bars and club nights. I play pop, dance, house, disco and hip-hop, and I shape each set around who is in the room. A wedding with three generations dancing sounds different from a club night at 1am, and I plan for both.

Music brings me a lot of joy, and what I love most is giving that to a room full of people who want to dance. Before any booking I ask what you love and what you can't stand, then I build the night from there.

Weddings, club nights and parties

Whatever the night, the aim is the same: a dance floor that stays full from the first song to the last.

Weddings

Weddings are some of my favourite gigs. I've played plenty over the years, and I still love the part where a couple and I build a night that feels like them, not a generic playlist.

A good wedding set has a shape. I start gently, read the room as it warms up, and bring the energy up in stages until the floor is full and nobody wants it to end. Nearly 20 years behind the decks means I know when to hold back and when to go for it.

Your music, planned with you

We agree your must-plays, your do-not-plays and your first dance before the day.

Every generation on the floor

From Motown and disco to current pop and house, so your grandparents, your friends and your cousins all have something to dance to.

A shape to the night

The energy is built in phases, so the floor fills up naturally instead of peaking too early.

How it works

  1. Get in touch. Tell me the date, the venue and roughly how many guests.
  2. We talk through the night. The music you love, what to avoid, your first dance and any moments you want soundtracked.
  3. I plan the shape of the evening. Then it's yours to approve or tweak.
  4. On the night, I read the room. The plan is the starting point, and I adapt to how the crowd is feeling.
