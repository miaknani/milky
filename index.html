Hi, I'm MILKY

I've been obsessed with music since I was young. A school friend nicknamed me DJ because I was always the one making the playlists for parties and the hockey bus, and it stuck.

I'm a queer DJ in Glasgow and I've been behind the decks for almost 20 years. I used to run my own club nights here, and I've played hundreds of parties, weddings, bars and club nights. I play pop, dance, house, disco and hip-hop, and I shape each set around who is in the room. A wedding with three generations dancing sounds different from a club night at 1am, and I plan for both.

Music brings me a lot of joy, and what I love most is giving that to a room full of people who want to dance. Before any booking I ask what you love and what you can't stand, then I build the night from there.
